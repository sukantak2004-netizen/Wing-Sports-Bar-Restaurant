The Journey

HOW IT STARTED

From a borrowed fryer to the busiest game-day bar in Austin. Every step mattered.

2015

The Backyard Beginning

Marco and Elena started frying wings in a borrowed backyard fryer for friends and fantasy football Sundays. Word spread fast — faster than they could cook.

2016

The First Location

A 900-square-foot space on East 6th opened with 6 tables, 3 sauces, and one TV. Opening night sold out in 90 minutes. The dream was alive.

2018

The Sauce Wall

We launched our house-made sauce program — 12 sauces, all made in-house daily. The Reaper Challenge was born and quickly became legendary.

2020

Pandemic Pivot

We pivoted to takeout and delivery to keep our team employed. Our community showed up for us — and we never forgot it. Loyalty runs both ways.

2022

The Stadium Drive Move

We moved into our current home on Stadium Drive — 17 screens, 32 taps, a full bar, and room for the whole crew. The game-day headquarters was born.

2024

Voted #1 in Austin

Austin Chronicle readers voted us the #1 wings and #1 sports bar in the city. We celebrated by giving away 1,000 free wings to our regulars. Best. Day. Ever.

Today

Still Family-Owned

Marco and Elena still run the show. Same recipes, same values, same obsession with doing it right. Come in and say hi — they're probably at the bar.
