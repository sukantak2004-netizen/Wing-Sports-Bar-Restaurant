Good to Know

EVENT FAQ

Walk-ins are always welcome, but for marquee events like NFL Sundays, UFC pay-per-views, and Reaper Challenge Night, we strongly recommend reserving a table — especially for groups of 4 or more. Reservations are free and can be made through our contact page.
Most weekly events — trivia, karaoke, DJ nights, NFL Sundays — have no cover charge. The Reaper Challenge has a $10 entry fee (which includes the wings), and special monthly events like Comedy Night may have a cover. Watch-party packages are priced separately.
Absolutely. Our packages are built for private groups: The Front Row (1–4 guests), The Sports Box (6–10 guests), and The Owner's Box (10–25 guests). Each includes reserved seating, dedicated screens, food, and drinks. Full buyouts of the bar are also available on request.
Doors open at 10am for Game Day Brunch — one hour before the 1pm ET kickoffs. We recommend arriving by 11:30am on busy Sundays, especially during playoffs. Reservations guarantee your table until 30 minutes after the first kickoff.
Usually yes — we carry NFL Sunday Ticket, NBA League Pass, MLB Extra Innings, NHL Center Ice, UFC PPVs, and Premier League. For niche or international events, contact us at least 48 hours in advance and we'll do our best to get it on a screen.
